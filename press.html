A deep, retro space tower defense. Easy to pick up, hard to master. Endless runs. No ads.

Description

Last Gradient is a hard, endless tower defense set in deep space. Every run starts simple: build towers and hold the core. Then the systems stack up. Towers, talents, Fusions, strains and bosses all feed into each other, and that is where the skill cap lives.

You pay once and the whole game is yours. The game has no ads, no in-app purchases and no tracking.

What Is Different

  • The core flies. Tap Lift Off, pick a landing spot, and the swarm reroutes to follow. Land on a tower and the core takes its gun.
  • The swarm reads the build. Every few waves the swarm studies the defense and sends a strain built to beat it. Whatever ends a run comes back next time as the Nemesis.
  • The map fights back. Storms boost an element. A Black Hole eats towers: feed it the swarm and it bursts into a White Hole.
  • Thirteen bosses. Five are hive monsters, like the Titan. The others are stranger: the Immortal Snail, The Auditor, and Leia, who is based on my cat.
  • Music composed live. The game has no playlist. It composes its music from 40 styles across ten genres and follows the fight. In the Music Studio, players write their own tracks and share them as a short code.
  • Deep builds. Thirteen base towers and seven talent trees, each with a Capstone that changes how the game plays.
  • One Daily Hunt. One map a day. Every pilot gets the same talent build and the same big dice rolls.

About the Developer

I'm not a coder, and I'm not an artistic person. I'm a gamer with a really good understanding of game mechanics. I built Last Gradient together with AI. We worked on the code, the art and the music side by side. The decisions were mine, and I closely criticized every output until it felt right.

I have a day job. I made this game because I'm tired of mobile games full of ads and microtransactions. The full letter is on the front page, under Why I Made It.
