The game

What Is In It

  • Lift Off

    The core is your base. It can Lift Off and land in a new spot. If it lands on a tower, it docks and fires that tower's weapon at ×2 damage.

  • Towers and Talents

    19 towers and 7 Talent trees. Every tower has upgrades, affixes and veteran ranks. Different builds play differently.

  • Enemies That Adapt

    Enemy strains evolve against the towers you build. A boss arrives every 10 waves.

  • Endless Waves

    A run continues until the core is destroyed. Zones, wormholes and events change the board during a run.

  • Daily Hunt

    One new board each day. Every player gets the same 40-point build.

  • Generated Music

    The game composes its soundtrack while you play. There are 13 boss themes.

From the developer

Why I Made It

I'm not a coder, and I'm not an artistic person. I'm a gamer with a really good understanding of mechanics, and I had an idea to make a game for the players.

I made Last Gradient with AI. AI wrote the code, the art and the music. The design, the mechanics and every decision are mine. I don't have a team to help.

I'm not here to make a buck. I have a day job. I'm tired of the cancerous games on the market today. You know the microtransaction-riddled crap I'm talking about.

This is a letter to the games of old, the ones you just played and enjoyed. The games that developers spent time on to make great. I want to detach myself from the corporate slop that gaming has become.

I wanted a game anyone can pick up and play offline, while waiting in line or to unwind, without ads inside the game.

I plan to take player feedback and continually improve the game. Send Feedback is in the game, and I read the reports.

Nic
