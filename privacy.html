Privacy

Last updated November 12, 2026

Last Gradient is made by one person. This page says what the website and the game collect. The short version: an email address if you sign up, and a report if you send feedback. Nothing else.

The Website

  • Email sign-up. If you enter your email address, I store it and the time you signed up. I use it to send you one email when the game launches. I do not sell it or share it.
  • No tracking. The website has no ads, no analytics and no cookies.
  • Hosting. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub can log technical details of a visit, such as your IP address.
  • Fonts. The fonts load from Google Fonts, so your browser makes a request to Google to get them.

The Game

  • No account. The game does not ask for your name, email address or a login.
  • Your save stays on your device. Progress and settings are stored on your device. They are not sent to me.
  • No ads and no analytics. The game does not track how you play.
  • Send Feedback. A report is sent only when you write one and tap Send. It contains what you wrote, your name if you chose to add it, the game version, your device type, the screen size and the log of your last run. I use reports to fix bugs and improve the game.
  • Hosting and fonts. The web version of the game is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its fonts from Google Fonts, the same as the website.

Where It Is Stored

Sign-ups and feedback reports are stored in Google Sheets inside the Google Workspace account for lastgradient.com. Only I can open them.

Your Choices

To have your email address or a feedback report removed, email nic@lastgradient.com. I will delete it.

Children

The website and the game are not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If what the website or the game collects changes, I will update this page and the date at the top.
