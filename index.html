The game

What You Are Getting Into

Every run starts simple: build towers, hold the core. Then the systems stack up. Towers, talents, Fusions, strains and bosses all feed into each other, and that is where the skill cap lives.

  • Your Base Can Fly

    Your core is not bolted down. Tap Lift Off, pick a landing spot, and the swarm reroutes to follow you. Land on a tower and the core takes its gun: ×2 damage and +1 range, while its own gun keeps firing.

  • Builds That Play Differently

    19 towers with upgrades, affixes and veteran ranks. 7 Talent trees, each with a Capstone that changes how you play. Put 8 points into two trees to open their Fusion. There are 21, like Hot Rounds: Kinetic hits set enemies on fire.

  • A Swarm That Adapts

    Every few waves the swarm reads your defense and sends a strain built to beat it. You get one wave of warning, and selling is free until it lands. Whatever ends your run comes back next time as your Nemesis.

  • Endless Waves

    A run lasts until your core falls. A boss arrives every 10 waves, each with phases and its own theme. Wormholes open new roads, and 21 Anomalies trade power for risk, like Glass Sky: +35% damage, but no core shield.

  • Daily Hunt

    One map a day, with the same talent build for every player. Your first run counts. Every bounty you claim is a choice: Extract and end the run as a win, or push on for ×1.25 score.

  • Music That Writes Itself

    The soundtrack is composed live while you play, so it is never the same twice. Each of the 13 bosses has its own theme. Open the Music Studio to change the genre, mix the parts or write your own tracks.

From the developer

Why I Made It

I'm not a coder, and I'm not an artistic person. I'm a gamer with a really good understanding of game mechanics, and I had an idea to make a game for the players.

I built Last Gradient together with AI. We worked on the code, the art and the music together. The decisions were mine, and I closely criticized the outputs. I don't have a team to help.

I'm not here to get rich. I have a day job. I'm tired of the predatory games on the market today. You know the microtransaction-riddled crap I'm talking about.

This game serves as a letter to the games of old, the ones you just played and enjoyed. The games that developers spent time on to make great. I want to detach myself from the corporate slop that gaming has become.

I wanted a game anyone can pick up and play offline, while waiting in line or to unwind, without ads inside the game.

I plan to take player feedback and continually improve the game. Send Feedback is in the game, and I read the reports.

Nic
