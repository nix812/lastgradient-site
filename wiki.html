How a Run Works

Enemies walk the roads to your core. You build towers to stop them. A run lasts until your core falls, a boss reaches the core, or you choose to Extract with a win.

Build
Tap an empty tile, pick a tower, and tap again to build. Towers near a bend in the road keep enemies in range longer.
Next
Tap Next to start the wave. The next wave waits for a clear field, up to a cap. Calling a wave early pays for each enemy still alive.
Credits
Kills pay credits. Spend them on towers, upgrades, the core and the probe.
Power
Every tower draws 1 power. Your grid holds 8, plus 1 every 3 waves. When the grid is full, upgrade the towers you have.
Bosses
A boss arrives every 10 waves. If a boss reaches the core, the run ends.
Threat Board
The Threat chip in the top bar shows the next wave, what beats it, the coming Strain Assault and what the swarm is breeding. Tap any enemy to see what it is and what beats it.
Extract or Push
Kill the Most Wanted boss to claim its bounty. Then Extract to end the run as a win, or Push on for more score.
Saved runs
The game saves when you close it. A saved run resumes at the start of the wave you left. A Daily Hunt resumes at the exact moment.

The Core

The core holds the Last Gradient. The core is your base, your biggest gun, and the one thing you cannot lose.

Lift Off
The core flies to any open tile in the sector, or onto an attack tower. Every road re-routes to the landing spot, and enemies already walking turn toward it. In the air the gun is offline and the shield is down. Lift Off takes 3 waves to recharge after a landing. The Uplink shortens that.
Docking
Land on an attack tower and the core docks it as a second gun: its element, affixes, spec, perks and Technique, at ×2 Damage and +1 Range. The core's own gun keeps firing. Support towers cannot be docked.
Core Link
The landed core links its 3 nearest attack towers within 4 tiles: +10% Damage and attack rate for each tower, and +5% Damage for the core per link.
Move
Any tower can Move from its panel for a small fee and keep its upgrades. Moves are free at a checkpoint.
Battery
The core's gun. 12 levels. At level 4, Twin Battery: Fires a second shell at the next target. At level 8, Siege Shells: +0.6 blast radius, +25% damage to elites and bosses. At level 12, Orbital Uplink: Every 5th volley calls a strike on the toughest enemy in range.
Shield
Absorbs leak damage before the hull, and recharges when left alone.
Hull
10 levels. Each level adds 12% max hull and takes 5% off leak damage. A late-run place to spend credits.
Uplink
9 levels. Each level cuts ability cooldowns by 5%. At level 3, Double Tap: Orbital Strike hits the same spot twice. At level 6, Undertow: Enemies leave Stasis slowed 40% for 4s. At level 9, Nova Echo: Entropy Nova pulses again 1.5s later at half strength.
Core light
The core's light reveals cloaked enemies near it. The light dims as the core loses health. Each Supernova widens it by 1 tile, up to 5.
Red Alert
A boss within 8 tiles of the core. The field turns red and the core goes to Last Stand: its shells deal ×2.5 to that boss, and it fires ×1.6 as fast.
Fog of war
Ground you have not seen is dark. The core, your towers and the probe light it up.

Power and Placement

Power grid
Every tower draws 1 power. Capacity is 8 plus 1 every 3 waves. The reactor adds more for credits.
Overcharge
Spend spare power on one tower: +35% Damage and +15% Attack Speed per point, up to 3. Free to change at any time.
Power Node
A glowing cyan diamond. A tower on a Power Node draws no power and gets +25% Damage and +15% Range. The core on a Node gets +50% Damage and +20% attack rate. Roads never pave over a Node.
Node Link
Attack towers on Power Nodes within 9 tiles of each other link up. Each gets +10% Damage, and the beam between them burns anything that crosses it.
Resonance
Towers of different elements side by side: +8% Damage per element.
No-Build Zone
Nothing can be built within 4 tiles of a wormhole.
Ground-only
The Flame Projector, Gravity Well, Buzzsaw and Rimewall cannot hit fliers.
IDLE
A tag on a tower that has not fired for three waves. Move it to where the enemies are.

Elements, Statuses and Combos

Every attack tower has an element. Enemies resist some elements and are weak to others, and elements combine.

Elements

Kinetic
Bullets, rails and blades. Crits and armor piercing.
Thermal
Fire. Applies Burn.
Cryo
Cold. Applies Chill, then Freeze.
Arc
Lightning. Chains between enemies and applies Shock. Tears shields.
Void
Beams and gravity. Applies Mark.
Neutral
Support towers: they boost, pay or protect.

Statuses

Burn
Deals damage over time. Stops healing.
Chill
Slows. Stacks up to 5.
Freeze
At 5 Chill stacks the enemy is frozen in place.
Shock
The enemy takes +15% damage while Shocked.
Mark
Each stack adds +5% damage taken.
Stun
The enemy cannot move.
Cloaked
Cannot be targeted. Relay Pylons, the core's light and Sensor Sweep reveal cloaked enemies.
Plating
Blunts every hit except piercing fire: the Rail Lance, Void Lens beams, Burn and other damage over time, and abilities.
Shield
Absorbs damage before HP. Arc tears shields at 3×.

Combos

Shatter
Kinetic hit on a Frozen enemy deals burst damage.
Discharge
Voltaic: Arc hits charge an enemy, then release a share of its max HP.
Thaw Crack
Cryogenics: when a Freeze ends, the ice cracks for a share of max HP.
Thermal Shock
Thermal hit on a heavily Chilled enemy shatters the frost.
Overload
Thermal on Shocked or Arc on Burning causes an explosion.
Superconduct
Arc hit on a Chilled enemy spreads Shock nearby.
Collapse
Void hit at max Marks implodes the target.

Terms

Pierce
Hits pass through additional enemies.
Chain
Attacks jump to additional nearby enemies.
Splash
Damages enemies around the impact. Radius in tiles.
Crit
A critical hit. Deals bonus Crit Damage.
Ramp
Beam damage that grows while the beam holds one target.

Towers

There are 20 towers: 13 you unlock by reaching waves, and 7 that sit at the top of a Talent tree. Every element has two base towers.

How towers grow

Upgrade
Levels 1 to 5. Each level raises damage (×1, ×1.65, ×2.6, ×4.1, ×6.4) and rolls a random bonus, an affix.
Specialization
At LV 3 you pick one of two specs. The choice is permanent.
Ascend
Past LV 5 a tower Ascends: ×1.15 Damage (Void Lens ×1.08), a new affix and a perk you choose from 3.
Tier names
A tower's total level sets its tier color: Common (to 3), Uncommon (to 6), Rare (to 9), Epic (to 12), Legendary (to 15), Mythic (above 15).
Veteran
Towers that fight earn Veteran ranks, and a name of their own. Each rank offers a mutation: an affix you choose.
Technique
A second fire mode for one tower type, learned with a one-point talent. Switch it per tower from the tower panel. A Technique is a different job, not an upgrade.
Mastery
Every tower type ranks up across runs from its share of the damage. Ranks 1 to 5: Mastery badge on the build card; +5% Damage (support towers: +5% strength); Gold trim on the tower; Ascend offers 4 perks instead of 3; Its own legendary affix can roll.
Sell
A sold tower refunds 70% of what you put in. Selling is free before a Strain Assault lands, and refunds 100% at a checkpoint.

The towers below are grouped by element. In each group the base towers come first, in the order you unlock them, and the tier tower comes last. Spend 16 points in a Talent tree to unlock its tier tower.

Runs pay XP, and each level is a Talent point. There are seven trees. Talents are permanent between runs, and you can respec for free between runs and at checkpoints. Mid-run changes cost Recalibration tokens.

Rows
Each row of a tree opens after 3 points in that tree.
Signature
Every point in a tree raises its Signature, an effect only that tree has. Deeper points count more. The numbers below are at 12 points.
Fusion
8 points in each of two trees opens their Fusion. One Fusion is active in a run.
Capstone
12 points in a tree opens its three Capstones. A Capstone changes how a run plays, often with a cost. You hold 1 Capstone, 2 from Level 25 and 3 from Level 60.
Tier tower
16 points in a tree unlocks its tier tower.
Builds
Save up to five Talent layouts and load one between runs.

The Map

Wormhole
The origin of a road. New wormholes can open on any side of the core. A forming wormhole shows its future road for one wave, then comes a checkpoint, then it opens and the map grows. On Normal they open at Waves 15, 28, 42, 58, 78.
Checkpoint
Before a wormhole's first wave, once the field is clear, the next wave waits 90 s. The game keeps running: Lift Off has no cooldown, selling refunds 100%, tower moves are free and Talents respec for free. Start early for bonus credits and score.
Zone
The contract a run is fought under: which enemies come most, the weather, the events, the Most Wanted boss, plus a perk and a drawback. You pick one of three at the start, and a new one through a jump door at every bounty.
Jump door
After a bounty you can push on through one of three doors: a new zone for the next 20 waves. Steady (purse ×1.25: no extra danger), Hot (purse ×1.4: +12% enemy hp · +10% score), Deadly (purse ×1.6: +25% enemy hp, more elites · +25% score).
Escalation
From Wave 50, each boss wave makes you choose one of two permanent twists. They stack, and each one raises your score and XP.
Chaos
Calm (score ×0.8), Normal (score ×1), Unstable (score ×1.35), Pandemonium (score ×1.8). Higher Chaos means more events, storms and elites, a bigger map and a swarm that evolves sooner.

Black Hole

From Wave 20, about 3 to 5 times in 100 waves, on no set waves. A marker shows where a Black Hole will land, 8 seconds ahead.

The pull
The hole lands on your biggest build and pulls every tower within 12 tiles toward it, faster near the center. A tower that reaches the center is swallowed: 2 at first, up to 10 in later waves. A tower on a Power Node, or docked under the core, holds.
Mass
Every enemy killed within 12 tiles of the hole adds Mass (an elite adds 3), and so does every enemy that falls in. You have 45 seconds to fill it.
White Hole
Fill the Mass and the hole becomes a White Hole. Every tower it ate comes back one level higher, and a Newborn Star stays on the field and lifts the Damage of towers near it.
Starved
A hole that is not filled keeps your towers for 2 to 4 waves, scars the ground and spits Voidlings.
What to do
Bring the fight to it. Move your best towers off its center, keep the rest firing into its field, and save a Strike for a Gravity Bomb.

Training and the Field Manual

Training is optional. Basic Training is one guided run. The Field Manual holds a short lesson for every system, and each lesson pays XP once.

Basics

Towers and Elements
Every tower has an element: Kinetic, Thermal, Cryo, Arc or Void. Enemies resist some and are weak to others. When: Every build. The Threat Board says what the next wave is weak to.
Upgrades, Specs and Selling
Tap a built tower to open its panel. Upgrade raises its level: more damage and a random bonus (an affix). At LV 3 you pick one of two specs permanently. LV 5 is the highest level. After that, a tower can Ascend to gain a perk. A tower in the wrong spot can Move for a small fee and keep its upgrades, or be sold for 70% of what you put in. When: When power is full, or a tower already sits in the right spot.
Power, Reactor and Core
Each tower draws 1 power. Capacity grows by 1 every 3 waves; the reactor adds more for credits. The core has its own upgrades: Battery (its gun, and a docked tower), Shield, Hull and Uplink. When: When the power readout is full and you still have credits.
Lift Off and Docking
Your core can fly. Tap Lift Off, pick a landing spot and read the preview. Land on an attack tower to dock it as a second gun at ×2 damage and +1 range. The core’s own gun keeps firing. The landed core links its 3 nearest attack towers within 4 tiles (+10% damage and attack rate each). Any tower can Move from its panel. When: Whenever the swarm changes: a new road, a strain warded against your main element, a boss on the way. After a landing, Lift Off recharges for 3 waves.
Overcharge
Overcharge spends spare power on a tower: +35% damage per step, 1 power per step. When: When you have power left over and one tower doing most of the work.
Abilities and Charges
Five abilities on the left rail, each on its own cooldown. Strike hits an area, Stasis freezes everything, Frenzy speeds up your towers, Nova damages every enemy, Scan reveals cloaked enemies and marks the largest threat. When: On a dense pack, a boss, or a wave that is leaking.
Crates and Salvage
Drop ships leave crates on the map: credits, ability charges or tokens. Crates disappear after a while. Golden crates pay more. Cursed crates carry a penalty. When: From Wave 3, between and during waves.
Probe and Loot Sites
The probe sits on the core. Select it and tap a site to send it: it salvages a wreck, harvests a gas cloud or mines a rock, then flies home on its own and banks the cargo at the core. Red drones guard some sites and shoot it. Upgrades (Armor, Engine, Lance and more) make it tougher and let it fight back. When: From Wave 2, all run long.

Advanced

The Swarm Adapts
The swarm breeds against your defense. Enemies that get furthest pass on their genes (Plating, Shell, Ward, Haste…). Marks on each enemy show its genes. When: From Wave 8 on Normal. The Threat Board shows what it is breeding and why.
Bounties and the Purse
Each run posts a Most Wanted boss. Killing it claims the bounty. Some of your XP is held in the Bounty Purse. Extract deposits all of it and ends the run as a win. A jump door takes you to a new zone for a larger purse. If the core falls later, you keep 30%. When: Every 20 waves.
Veterans, Affixes, Resonance
Towers that fight earn veteran ranks; each rank offers a mutation. Affixes are the bonuses a tower rolls on upgrades. Towers of different elements side by side resonate: +8% damage each. When: Whenever the Ready button shows a count; whenever you place a tower.
Pylon and Forge
The Relay Pylon boosts damage and attack speed of towers in its range and reveals cloaked enemies. The Credit Forge pays credits every wave. When: Pylon: when towers are clustered. Forge: early in a long run.
Storms and Anomalies
Storms drift across the map and boost one element: towers of that element inside a storm glow and hit harder. Each storm also hits the enemies under it (lightning, burns, frost, Marks or a reveal), and your probe can harvest a storm for an ability charge. Kills fill the Entropy gauge; when it is full you draw an Anomaly, a permanent twist with an upside and a downside. When: Storms from Wave 6. Anomalies whenever the gauge fills.
Black Hole
A marker shows where a Black Hole will land, 8 seconds ahead. It lands on your biggest build and pulls every tower within 12 tiles toward it, faster near the center. A tower that reaches the center is swallowed: 2 at first, more in later waves. A tower on a Power Node, or docked under the core, holds. Every enemy killed within 12 tiles of it adds Mass, and so does every enemy that falls in (within 5 tiles). Fill its Mass in 45 seconds and it becomes a White Hole: a Newborn Star stays on the field and lifts the Damage of towers near it, and every tower it ate comes back one level higher. Starved, it keeps them for 2 to 4 waves, scars the ground and spits voidlings. A Strike aimed inside it becomes a Gravity Bomb that drags enemies in. When: From Wave 20, 3 to 5 times in 100 waves. No set waves.
Wormholes and Checkpoints
Every so often a wormhole opens a new road and the map grows. Nothing can be built within 4 tiles of a wormhole. The wave before it lands is a checkpoint: the next wave waits 90 s. Full refunds, free tower moves, a free respec and Lift Off with no cooldown until it starts. When: At set waves (Normal: 15, 28, 42…).
Strain Assaults
Every few waves the swarm reads your defense and sends one strain built to beat it. It is announced a wave ahead, and selling is free (a refit) until it lands. If you hold it, the strain is defeated: +1 power and a bounty. When: Normal: from Wave 14, every 8 waves.
Bosses, Boons and Nemesis
A boss lands every 10 waves. It has phases and powers, and if it reaches the core the run ends. Killing one pays a boon: one of three permanent bonuses. A boss or strain that ends your run becomes your Nemesis and comes back next run. When: Wave 10, 20, 30…
Black Market and Cash In
From Wave 10 the Score button opens the Exchange: the Black Market sells three items (restocked after boss waves), and Cash In turns credits into score. When: When credits build up and the grid is full.
The Remnant
From Wave 28 the Remnant march with the swarm: war machines left by the pilots before you. Each has one trick. A Husk stands back up once unless area damage, Burn or an ability finishes it. A Bulwark’s front plate blocks shots from ahead. A Welder repairs the others. A Lodestone drags bolts in and shrugs them off; Arc hurts it double. A Siege Crawler plants and shells a tower offline. An Adapter armours up against the element that hit it last. A Warden’s squad takes less damage near it. When: From Wave 28 on Normal. Mission Control names each one the first time you meet it.

Endgame

Techniques
A Technique is an alternate fire mode for one tower type, learned with a one-point talent. Each tower then has a Fire row: Standard or the Technique. It is a different job, not an upgrade. When: After you learn one in Talents.
Ascend Perks and Mastery
Past LV 5 a tower Ascends: ×1.15 damage and a choice of three perks that change how it works (a forking beam, ricochets, shattering freezes). Every tower type also has a Mastery: 5 ranks earned across runs from its share of the damage. When: Late in a run, when your best towers are maxed. Mastery: after every run.
Daily Hunt
Every day has one Daily Hunt: the same map, zone, bounty, Nemesis, talent build and dice for everyone. The build has two Capstones and their towers. Your first run of the day is ranked: best wave first, then score. When: Once a day, from the hangar. Opens at Level 20 and stays open after a Supernova.
Talents and Progression
Runs pay XP (from score and the Bounty Purse). Each level is a talent point. Seven trees: rows open every 3 points, a Capstone at 12 changes how you play. Supernova resets your level and talents for a permanent bonus, an emblem and a wider core light. Your career rank counts every XP you have ever earned, and a Supernova never lowers it. Each rank also needs days served. The Archive keeps what you have met. When: Between runs.
Talents Deep Dive
Every point in a tree also raises its Signature, an effect only that tree has; deeper points count more. 8 points in each of two trees opens their Fusion: 21 in all, one active per run. 12 points in a tree opens a Capstone, a new way to play, and 16 its tier tower. When: Between runs, once you have points in two trees.
