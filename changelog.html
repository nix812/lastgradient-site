What changed · game version 6.20.0 · updated November 26, 2026

Changelog

Every change to the game, newest first, in plain words. I want you to be able to see what is being done to the game and why. The list goes back to the first builds in October 2026, when the game was called Entropy. Small fixes are grouped, and a few early versions are missing from my records. The game is not released yet: these versions went to me and a few testers.

Training

November 25 to 26, 2026
6.20.0Nov 26
Training
  • Training cards hold still. A card takes one place and stays there. It slides only when its target comes under it. Before, a card took the place nearest its target on every frame, so it jumped as the probe flew: 38 jumps in one Basic Training run, now 4.
  • Training camera: it glides to a target in a third of a second and no longer chases the probe. It frames the probe with the place the probe is flying to.
  • Basic Training: the core's gun stays off in Waves 1 and 2, so the towers you build do the work. It comes on at Wave 3, with the core lesson.
  • Three lesson lines corrected: the Support towers intro, the Relay Pylon card and the Entropy gauge.
  • Support towers: the Relay Pylon and the Snare Beacon no longer level up with the towers around them. You upgrade every tower yourself.
  • Upgrade arrow: it keeps the same share of the tower at every zoom, skips support towers, and grows the longer an upgrade waits. A veteran star stays small when you zoom out.
  • Bosses: oddball bosses wait until Wave 40. From your second Supernova they come from Wave 20. Quackzilla is now The Quacken.
  • Daily Hunt: it opens once your best run reaches Wave 40.
  • Run log: each wave now lists what hit and what healed the core, and frame time is split into game rules and drawing.
  • New icon: an hourglass of light inside a tilted ring.

Squall Array

November 22 to 24, 2026
6.19.0Nov 24
Journey
  • Music: the default mix is now Journey. It plays all ten genres and picks one to fit the battle when a song starts. A quiet field gets Ambient, Lo-fi, Dub and Trip Hop. Later waves bring Synthwave, Chiptune and House, and hard fights reach Trance and Techno. No genre returns until three others have played. Before, the default mix was three slow genres.
  • Music Studio: a Journey chip in Listen. A profile that picked its own genres keeps them.
  • Arc Coil: +1 Chain at level 3 and +1 at level 5. A chain hit 4 enemies however large the pack was, so the Coil fell behind after Wave 15.
6.18.2Nov 24
  • Supernova: the Back and Next buttons sit in one row at the bottom of the Remnant and Cosmic Law steps. They were stacked on the left.
6.18.1Nov 24
  • The Supernova card is laid out again: its three steps show at the top, what you give up, keep and gain sit in three panels, and the requirements are check marks beside the button.
6.18.0Nov 24
Motion
  • Smooth motion. Enemies, shots, the probe and the core now glide. The game worked out positions 30 times a second and drew 60 frames, so everything moved in small jumps.
  • Ground enemies bob with their steps: a Chilled one trudges, a fast one scuttles. Bosses stomp. An enemy dragged back by a Gravity Well keeps facing forward.
  • Boss deaths take longer. The body shakes and cracks for 1.2 seconds before the blast, and the boon card comes 2.4 seconds after the kill. It was 1 second.
  • Storm timer. An arc on a storm's rim runs down with the time the storm has left. It turns white and blinks in the last 6 seconds.
6.17.6Nov 24
  • A stocked ability now shows its recharge with the same clockwise sweep as an empty one.
6.17.5Nov 23
  • Balance: the Squall Array deals 10 damage a hit, down from 12. The Cryogenics tree still lifts it, so a Cryo build gets the stronger tower.
6.17.4Nov 23
  • The run log now names the rules a run started on. A saved run keeps the rules it began with, and the log could not show that before.
6.17.3Nov 23
  • A stocked ability shows a recharge bar and the seconds left. Before, the button showed nothing while a charge came back.
6.17.2Nov 23
  • Abilities: only the last charge you spent recharges. Hold 5 Strikes and fire 1, and the cooldown brings you back to 5. Fire 3, and you recharge to 3.
6.17.1Nov 22
  • Abilities: a banked stack of charges recharges. Before, the cooldown only ran when you were below your charge slots.
6.17.0Nov 22
Squall Array
  • New tower: the Squall Array, a second Cryo base tower. It casts a blizzard on the biggest pack in its range. Every enemy inside takes cold damage and Chill, fliers too. It unlocks at Wave 18. Every element now has two base towers.
  • The build menu shows five towers a row.

Ranks, the Black Hole and the Hive

November 15 to 22, 2026

Feature freeze began on November 15. From here the work is polish, bugs and text, with a few exceptions I judged worth it.

6.16.10Nov 22
  • A selected probe with no order lets go after 3 seconds. It was 6.
6.16.9Nov 22
  • The screen keeps its size when you leave the game and come back. The layout is now sized a new way, after three earlier fixes did not hold on iPhone.
6.16.8Nov 22
  • Hotfix: the Hangar ran off the bottom of the screen on iPhone and cut off Launch.
6.16.7Nov 21
  • Safer probe controls. With the probe selected, a tap on a site, a storm or the core still sends it at once. A tap on open ground drops a marker, and a tap on the marker sends the probe. This stops the probe flying off by accident.
6.16.6Nov 21
  • The page can no longer zoom, and a zoom can never resize the layout.
6.16.5Nov 21
  • Fix: the whole interface could come back about 19% short, with black below it, until the game was reopened.
6.16.4Nov 21
Career card
  • New: the Career card. It counts kills, bosses, elites, damage by element and time played, with normal runs and Daily Hunts kept apart. Open it from your rank in the Hangar.
6.16.3Nov 21
  • Training is optional and no longer has its own rank. Cadet, Pilot, Ace and Commander are gone. Lessons still pay XP.
6.16.2Nov 20
  • Every Training lesson was checked against the new rules and brought up to date, the Black Hole lesson most of all.
6.16.1Nov 20
Ranks
  • Career ranks. Your rank counts every XP you have ever earned, and a Supernova never takes it away. Each rank also needs days served, so nobody rushes the ladder. A rank you have not earned stays hidden until you get there.
  • Supernova rewards. Each Supernova gives an emblem, widens your core's light by 1 tile (up to 5) and unlocks a new color for the core. The Daily Hunt stays open after a Supernova.
  • The Black Hole is new. It lands on your biggest build and pulls towers within 12 tiles toward it. Towers that reach the center are swallowed. Feed it Mass before it closes: kill enemies within 12 tiles of it. Fed, it becomes a White Hole, gives your towers back one level higher and leaves a Newborn Star. Starved, it keeps them for 2 to 4 waves. A Strike aimed inside it becomes a Gravity Bomb.
  • The hive. Every swarm enemy is redrawn as a hive creature and renamed: Gnawer, Shellback, Skimmer, Clutch, Nurse, Bonewall, Mirrorshell, Matron, Bile Tick. Elites are now Overgrown: covered in glowing spines, with no crown.
  • Every sprite in the game is redrawn at 1.5 times the detail: all towers, the Remnant, the hive and all 13 bosses. The five regular bosses now match the hive.
  • The Wormhole Worm: if the Worm reaches your core, the end screen reads "Digested" and lists the towers it was carrying.
  • Talents: the last rank of the weakest talents now does something new, like Stagger, Cinders, Jumper and Lockdown. Read them in Talents.
  • Waves: the next wave waits for a clear field, up to a cap. Calling a wave early pays for each enemy still alive.
  • Wormholes: a new wormhole is found one wave before it opens, and it opens only after its checkpoint. A checkpoint starts only on a clear board.
  • Matrons: one Matron can hold only 6 of its own Gnawers at a time, and the field 36. Before, Matrons flooded long runs.
  • Sound: long runs keep their sound. Late-run battle sounds are thinned so the mix stays clear. Music levels are even across styles, and a full music audit removed long runs of piercing high notes.
  • The screen stays awake while the game is in front.
  • Training: lessons pay XP in joke numbers (4, 8, 15, 16, 23, 42 for Basics; 3.14, 42, 69, 420 and friends for Advanced, adding up to 1337.14).
  • The run log keeps the run in progress and the last 4 finished runs.
  • Saves: the game warns you if the device has no room to save. Codes pasted from other players are checked before they run.
  • Fixes: a shaking battlefield when starting an Advanced Training lesson, a thin red line from the Meteor Shower, the zone banner sitting on top of news lines.
6.16.0Nov 15
The Remnant
  • New enemy family: the Remnant, the war machines of the pilots before you, still running. Seven units, each with one trick: Husk (stands back up once), Bulwark (front plate blocks shots from ahead), Welder (repairs the others), Lodestone (pulls bolts and missiles in), Siege Crawler (plants and shells a tower offline), Adapter (resists the last element that hit it) and Warden (leads a squad).
  • The Remnant disrupt towers but never destroy them. They resist Kinetic by 20% and take 20% more from Arc.
  • This was the last feature update before launch.

Daily Dice, the New Font and the Keystone Core

November 12 to 14, 2026
6.15.2Nov 14
  • Tower moves are free at a checkpoint.
6.15.1Nov 14
  • Bug sweep. A support tower hit by an EMP could stay off for good. I then checked the whole game for the same kind of timer bug and fixed what turned up, the Worm included.
  • Incident: the first upload of this version did not load for about 2 minutes. A fixed copy replaced it.
6.15.0Nov 14
Pilot Certification
  • Training: every lesson now has a check you must pass, and lessons pay XP. Three new lessons.
  • The keystone core. The core is now the biggest thing on the board: a fortress deck with four bastions that does not turn, and the reactor on top. Twelve lights show the Battery level.
  • The core's light reveals cloaked enemies near it, and the light dims as the core loses health.
  • The Black Hole hunts crowds instead of drifting at random.
  • Checkpoints no longer freeze the game. The field keeps running, the next wave waits 90 seconds, and Lift Off is free and flies for real.
  • The Mirrorshell (then called the Reflector) now really deflects Pulse bolts, Void Lens beams and Arc chains. Its deflection had never worked.
  • Gravity: every pull back along the road spends an enemy's allowance, so Gravity Wells can take back at most half of an enemy's movement over time. Before, enough wells could stop the swarm completely.
  • Storms act: each storm hits the enemies under it, and the probe can harvest a storm for an ability charge.
  • The probe flies home on its own after it finishes the site you sent it to.
  • One font everywhere: Oxanium. The pixel logo stays.
  • Ability buttons show a cooldown sweep over the icon with the seconds left.
  • Pause menu rebuilt so every button fits on an iPhone in landscape.
  • Damage numbers show for crits only.
  • Music: fewer long, piercing high notes.
6.13.1Nov 13
  • The story intro plays no interface sounds.
6.13.0Nov 13
  • The Daily Hunt opens at Level 20, with a one-time briefing that explains what the Hunt is.
  • Balance, tuned with about 1,000 simulated runs. Command was too strong alone: Uplink, Telemetry and Tactician are smaller. Singularity was too strong alone: Crushing Depth is 20% smaller. Voltaic was too weak: Arc hits now charge an enemy until it discharges for a share of its max HP. Cryogenics was the weakest tree: a Freeze that ends now cracks the ice for a share of max HP.
  • Shock or a Mark now delays the Phase Wraith's next jump.
  • Fix: a Buzzsaw blade in flight kept running along the old road after the core moved. The blade now breaks up when the core lifts off.
6.12.0Nov 13
Shared Daily dice
  • In a Daily Hunt every pilot now gets the same big dice rolls that day (affixes, Anomaly offers and the rest). Before, affix luck alone could end the same player's run in 9 of 36 tries. A Daily bounty also pays 1 Recalibration token.
6.11.7Nov 12

6.11.6Nov 12
  • Basic Training locks Next, Lift Off, Strike and Probe until the lesson that teaches each one.
6.11.5Nov 12
  • The title screen's galaxies are drawn as slow, smooth light trails.
6.11.4Nov 12

6.11.3Nov 12

Fusion, Afterglow and the Story

November 7 to 12, 2026
6.10.0Nov 12
Fusion
  • The story. A short intro tells you what the Last Gradient is. It plays once, and you can watch it again in the Field Manual.
  • Fusions. Spend 8 points in each of two Talent trees to open their Fusion. Each of the 21 pairs has its own, and one is active in a run.
  • Signatures. Every Talent tree has a Signature: one effect only that tree has. Every point makes it stronger, and deeper points count more.
  • The Talents screen is new. Each talent is a picture, a whole tree fits on one screen, and a track shows what opens at 3, 6, 9, 12 and 16 points.
  • Send Feedback: report a bug or share an idea from the pause menu, the end-of-run screen or Settings.
  • The game starts offline, and carries its own fonts.
  • New players: the Daily Hunt and the deeper New Run options stay hidden until your first run or Training.
  • About 120 lines of game text now use full sentences and plain words. "Tutorial" is "Training" everywhere.
  • Settings has a Legal card.
6.8.1Nov 12
The Story
  • First version of the story intro, after early testers asked what the Last Gradient is.
6.8.0Nov 10
Afterglow
  • A look, sound and feel pass. Bloom lighting, death pops with debris and scorch marks, enemies that warp in, towers that drop in, health-bar trails, a staged boss death, a core destruction scene and a low-hull pulse at the screen edge.
  • One interface sound for every tap, 7 sounds for the probe, and muffled music on the pause menu.
  • Screens fade, panels step in and the end screen counts up.
  • Basic Training fast-forwards the wait while the first waves walk into range.
6.7.0Nov 9
Same Build
  • The Daily Hunt gives every pilot the same random 40-point Talent build, with 2 Capstones and their towers.
  • New Training text, and US spelling throughout.
6.6.1Nov 7
  • Sell takes two taps.
  • A quick tap on an underlined word inside a button now picks the button; a hold shows the tooltip.
  • Bigger tap areas on phone rails, clearer edge arrows and boss theme fixes.
  • Leak warnings name Matron drops.

The Hunt
  • Bounties win runs. Bring in the Most Wanted boss, then Extract for +50% XP or Push on for ×1.25 score toward the next bounty 20 waves later.
  • Your Nemesis: whatever ends a run comes back in the next one.
  • The Daily Hunt: one map, one bounty and one build for everyone. Your first run counts.
  • Every input is recorded, so a run can be replayed exactly and shared as a hunt code.
  • Veteran Picks: one card walks through every tower waiting for a pick.
  • Bullets whose target dies fly on and spark out instead of vanishing.
